- [[Overflow]] occurs when the left most bit is 1 for positive no. and 0 for negative no. , when the leftmost retained bit of pattern  is no the same as infinite number of digits to the left. follwing is from claude:  
	### concrete example (4-bit registers, for simplicity)
	Say you add two positive 4-bit numbers:

```
  0111   (+7)
+ 0001   (+1)
------
  1000
```

	The true mathematical answer is +8. Infinitely extended, +8 is `...0000 1000` — leftmost bits should be 0. But in our 4-bit box, the leftmost retained bit is `1`. That mismatch (expected 0, got 1) tells you: **overflow occurred**. The register now misleadingly looks like a negative number (-8 in 4-bit two's complement), even though the true answer was positive.
